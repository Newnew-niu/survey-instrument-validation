# Questionnaire / 问卷

40 statements, 5-point agreement scale (1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree). All items are scored so that a higher score means more difficulty. Original language: Chinese. English translation by the author.

40 道题，李克特 5 点计分（1 = 很不符合 … 5 = 很符合）。所有题目均为得分越高、困难程度越高。


## Mental health / 心理健康

| Item | English | 中文原题 |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | I always feel short of time and rush everything, e.g. crossing on a red light, walking and talking fast. | 你总觉得时间紧张，所以分秒必争。时间观念混乱。例如，过马路时冲红灯，走路和说话节奏很快。 |
| A2 | I often can't remember today something I planned yesterday. | 昨天想好的某件事今天怎么也记不起来了，而且经常出现这种情况。 |
| A3 | I feel low for no clear reason and often sit staring blankly. | 感到情绪有些抑郁，莫名情绪低沉，常静坐发呆。 |
| A4 | I no longer enjoy gatherings with friends as much as I used to; socialising feels like a duty. | 不像以前那样喜欢参加朋友的聚会，对各种娱乐没有兴趣，有勉强应酬的感觉。 |
| A5 | I am unusually sensitive to pollution and noise, crave quiet and lose my temper easily. | 对城市的污染以及噪声很敏感，比常人更渴望清净，且容易发火。 |
| A6 | I don't want to go into the lab or office; studying feels tiresome. | 不愿走进办公室，觉得工作（学习）令人厌倦。 |
| A7 | I worry about doing badly and care too much about how others judge my work. | 担心工作（学习）不好，过于在意别人对自己工作（学习）表现的评价。 |
| A8 | I avoid facing classmates and supervisors and tend to withdraw. | 不想面对同事（同学）和上司（老师），有一种自我封闭的倾向。 |
| A9 | After only a short time studying I feel exhausted and short of breath. | 每天工作（学习）很短时间，就感到身心倦怠，胸闷气短。 |
| A10 | I often can't sleep at night, and when I do sleep I dream constantly; my sleep is poor. | 晚上经常失眠。即使睡着也老是在做梦的状态中，睡眠质量很糟糕。 |
